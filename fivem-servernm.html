Server rules

Short version: stay in character, be kind out of character. Full rules are on Discord.

Respect every player

No harassment, hate speech, or targeting people out of character. If a scene stops being fun for someone, ease off.

No RDM or VDM

Random deathmatch and vehicle deathmatch are bannable. Violence needs a clear in-character reason and a build-up.

Value your life

Act like your character wants to survive. Don't pull a weapon on a cop and expect to walk away.

No exploits or cheats

Report bugs in a ticket. Using them for money or advantage gets you a permanent ban.

Get in the city in four steps

You need a legal copy of GTA V on PC. It takes about ten minutes.
